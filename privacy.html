Privacy Policy — Arka Labs Analytics

Last updated: 7 November 2026.

What data is accessed

Arka Labs Analytics accesses the owner's own YouTube channel data through Google's official YouTube APIs, using only these read-only OAuth scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly — view the YouTube account (channel and video information).
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly — view YouTube Analytics reports for the channel's content.

No other Google data is accessed. The app does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or any other Google service.

How the data is used

The data is used only to display the channel's own analytics (views, watch time, subscribers, retention and traffic sources) to the channel owner in a personal dashboard. The data is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and sharing

OAuth access tokens are stored securely and are used only to call the YouTube APIs on the owner's behalf. The data is never sold, shared with third parties, or transferred to anyone else. No data is shared with advertisers or analytics providers.

Data retention and deletion

Channel statistics are cached only to build the dashboard and are refreshed on each check. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately stops all access.
